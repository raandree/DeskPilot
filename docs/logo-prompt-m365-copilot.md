# Logo Prompt — DeskPilot (for Microsoft 365 Copilot / Designer)

Paste the **Prompt** section below into Microsoft 365 Copilot (or Microsoft
Designer / Image Creator). The **Context** section is supplementary briefing
material; you can paste it first, then the Prompt, or paste them together.

## Context — what DeskPilot is and what it lives next to

DeskPilot is a desktop-first, single-user AI assistant: a local PowerShell host
plus a static single-page web UI that drives a coding/agent engine on the
user's own machine. It is deliberately not cloud-first; the operator's desk —
their files, their shell, their projects — is the home.

DeskPilot is one of three sibling projects that share the same identity
family. The logo must visually fit alongside the other two:

- **ShellPilot** — the underlying engine (PowerShell agent runtime).
  DeskPilot is the cockpit; ShellPilot is the propulsion.
- **CopilotAtelier** — a curated catalogue of skills, instructions and agents
  for VS Code / `~/.copilot`. The atelier (workshop) where the craft is
  refined; DeskPilot consumes its outputs.
- **Agentic Operating Model (AOM)** — the umbrella training programme that
  frames all three (modules on agentic engineering, multi-agent orchestration
  and the Copilot Atelier). DeskPilot is positioned as the **friendly front
  door to the AOM**: the approachable surface that lets non-technical people
  practise the model without driving the tool stack. Honour that "welcoming
  entry point" idea in the mark.

Established visual identity to honour:

- **Palette** (already in the product): deep teal / petrol `#0d7d6e` as the
  primary accent on a light surface; a brighter teal `#2dd4bf` for dark mode;
  soft accent washes `#cbe8e3` (light) and `#123b37` (dark).
- **Mood**: calm, technical, slightly nautical (the "pilot" metaphor).
  Confident, not playful. Adult, not corporate.
- **Existing mark in the UI**: a single bold capital **`D`** in a rounded
  square swatch is used as the brand mark in the header — the new logo should
  either replace this gracefully or include a glyph that compresses to a
  recognisable single-letter mark at 20×20 px.

Family naming hint: every sibling ends in **-Pilot** or evokes a workshop /
operating-model. A consistent metaphor across the family — flight
instruments, control yokes, helms, compass roses, radar sweeps — is welcome
but not mandatory.

## Where the logo will live

The mark has two homes; design for both from the start.

1. **The GitHub repository** — the public face of the project.
   - A **README hero**: the primary lockup (glyph + "DeskPilot" wordmark) at
     the top of `README.md`, read on a screen ~600–900 px wide.
   - The **repository social-preview card** (1280 × 640 px) GitHub shows when
     the repo is shared in chat and on social platforms.
   - It must hold up on **both GitHub themes** — light and dark/dimmed — so
     supply a light-surface and a dark-surface variant.

2. **The DeskPilot entry page** — the first screen the user sees on launch.
   - The **in-app brand mark** in the header: today a bold capital `D` in a
     rounded-square swatch at roughly **20 × 20 px**. The new glyph must
     survive at that size and replace the `D` gracefully.
   - The browser-tab **favicon** at **16 × 16 px**.
   - A larger **start-screen lockup** shown while the local Host Server boots
     and on the empty-conversation welcome state, where there is room for the
     full glyph + wordmark.

Because DeskPilot is the **friendly front door to the Agentic Operating
Model**, the entry-page mark should read as a welcoming threshold — an
approachable cockpit a non-technical person is glad to sit down at — not a
piece of developer tooling.

## Prompt — copy this verbatim into Microsoft 365 Copilot

> Design a **logo for "DeskPilot"** — a desktop AI assistant that puts an
> agentic coding engine on the user's own machine.
>
> **Concept directions — give me 5 distinct proposals spanning these
> directions (you may mix or invent, but the 5 must be genuinely different
> marks, not recolours of one idea):**
>
> 1. *Cockpit-on-a-desk.* A minimalist desk surface seen from above, with a
>    pilot's control yoke or compass rose centred on it. Reads as both
>    "workspace" and "navigation".
> 2. *Letter D as a helm.* A sans-serif capital `D` whose counter (the
>    enclosed white space) is shaped like a stylised compass rose, helm wheel
>    or radar sweep. Must remain legible as a `D` at 16 px.
> 3. *Course indicator.* A horizon line crossed by a stylised pointer or
>    artificial-horizon glyph, framed in a rounded square — echoes the
>    existing brand mark in the app header.
> 4. *Front door / threshold.* An open doorway or portal whose opening forms a
>    compass needle, text cursor or `D` — expressing DeskPilot as the welcoming
>    entry point (front door) into the Agentic Operating Model.
> 5. *Pilot + desk monogram.* A tight `DP` (or a `D` cradling a small `P`)
>    built from a single geometric line weight, so it sits naturally beside a
>    future `SP` for ShellPilot and `CA` for CopilotAtelier.
>
> **Required style constraints:**
>
> - **Palette:** deep teal / petrol primary, hex `#0D7D6E`. A brighter teal
>   `#2DD4BF` for dark-mode and accent variants. White, near-black
>   (`#0E1B1F`) and a soft mint wash (`#CBE8E3`) are allowed as supporting
>   tones. **No** other hues.
> - **Geometry:** clean, geometric, single weight; rounded square frame
>   optional. Avoid gradients unless very subtle (single-stop, ≤10% lightness
>   shift). No drop shadows, no 3-D, no glows.
> - **Type, if any:** a modern geometric sans (DM Sans, Inter, Public Sans
>   style). The wordmark "DeskPilot" should be **one word, mixed case**, the
>   `P` capitalised. Tracking slightly tight.
> - **Sizing:** the mark must remain crisp and recognisable at **16 × 16 px**
>   (favicon) and at **20 × 20 px** (the in-app brand swatch — currently a
>   bold `D` in a rounded square). Submit a glyph-only variant suitable for
>   that swatch in addition to the full lockup.
>
> **Family fit (important):** DeskPilot is not a standalone product — it is the
> approachable cockpit of the **Agentic Operating Model**
> (github.com/raandree/AgenticOperatingModel), and it sits directly on top of
> **ShellPilot** (github.com/raandree/ShellPilot), the PowerShell engine that
> actually drives GitHub Copilot. The relationship is literal: **DeskPilot is
> the cockpit/desk; ShellPilot is the engine/propulsion.** A third sibling,
> **CopilotAtelier**, is the workshop where the craft is refined.
>
> Design the DeskPilot mark as the first member of a **coherent family**, so a
> future **ShellPilot** logo (engine / propulsion / shell prompt) and
> **CopilotAtelier** logo (workshop / atelier / craft) can reuse the same
> rounded-square frame, the same palette and the same line weight, swapping
> only the inner glyph. State a brief **system rule** (e.g. "all marks share
> one 24 px rounded square; the inner glyph is the only variable; DeskPilot's
> teal is the family base hue") so the set scales as more -Pilot tools join.
> Show how the **ShellPilot** sibling would look under that rule (a one-line
> sketch is enough) to prove the system works.
>
> **Deliverables — show all 5 proposals as thumbnails first; then, for the one
> I pick, produce the full set below:**
>
> 1. **Primary lockup**: glyph + "DeskPilot" wordmark, horizontal — the
>    **GitHub README hero**. Supply it on a **light** surface *and* a **dark**
>    surface (GitHub has both themes).
> 2. **Glyph-only mark**, 1:1, that fits the **20 × 20 px** in-app brand swatch
>    on the DeskPilot entry page (replacing today's bold `D`).
> 3. **Favicon-grade simplification** (**16 × 16 px**), anti-aliasing
>    intentionally minimised — show me what survives at that size.
> 4. **Repository social-preview card** (**1280 × 640 px**): the lockup centred
>    on a teal field, safe-area aware, for GitHub's share card.
> 5. **Start-screen / splash lockup**: a larger centred glyph + wordmark for
>    the DeskPilot launch and empty-conversation welcome state.
> 6. **Monochrome version** (single-colour: deep teal on white *and* white on
>    deep teal) for places where the full palette is unavailable.
> 7. A short **family-system note** (≤80 words) describing how **ShellPilot**
>    and **CopilotAtelier** slot into the same visual system.
>
> **Avoid:**
>
> - Aircraft / airline clichés (jet silhouettes, contrails).
> - Generic chat-bubble or robot-head iconography.
> - The Microsoft Copilot ribbon mark or anything that could be confused with
>   first-party Copilot branding.
> - Photorealism, mascots, faces.
>
> Show me **5 distinct proposals** first (as a labelled grid), then iterate on
> the strongest one.
